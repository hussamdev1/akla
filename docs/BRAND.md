# Akla — Brand Identity · أكلة

> **شنو ناكل اليوم؟ خلّها علينا.**
> *What are we eating today? Leave it to us.*

## 1. Brand core

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name (EN)** | Akla — written lowercase in the wordmark: `akla•` |
| **Name (AR)** | أكلة — "a meal / a dish". Descriptor: *رفيقك في كل أكلة* ("with you for every meal") |
| **Why the name** | Short, Arabic-native, easy to say in both languages, and it *is* the thing people are deciding on. |
| **Promise** | Make deciding what to eat the most fun part of the day. |
| **Short line** | *Decide less. Eat better.* — *فكّر أقل.. واستمتع أكثر* |
| **Market** | Kuwait first (KWD, 6 governorates, Arabic‑first); the whole GCC featured; usable in any country (local currency, time, language) |
| **Personality** | Playful · warm · confident · a little cheeky · always honest |
| **Not** | A corporate directory, a delivery app clone, a coupon spam site |

### The three verbs
1. **DISCOVER — اكتشف**: new places, cuisines and real deals.
2. **DECIDE — قرّر**: wheel, mood picker, games, group votes.
3. **ENJOY — استمتع**: motion, sound, confetti, streaks and badges.

## 2. Mascot — "Lugma · لقمة"
A round pomegranate‑red bun with **one bite taken out of its top‑right corner**, two dot eyes and a wide smile.
The bite is the brand's signature shape: it appears in the logo, favicon, the centre of the food wheel, loading
spinners and empty states. Lugma reacts to the user: it wobbles while the wheel spins, looks amazed on wins and
hungry on empty pages.

## 3. Logo
- **Symbol**: Lugma (bitten circle) — never mirrored in RTL.
- **Wordmark**: `akla` / `أكلة` in deep plum ink, Baloo Bhaijaan 2 ExtraBold, followed by the **bite dot** —
  a pomegranate circle with a sun‑yellow ring. The Arabic word is never split into coloured pieces (letters must keep joining).
- Clear space = the bite radius. Minimum size: 24 px symbol, 96 px lock‑up.

## 4. Colour system — "Pomegranate & Lime"

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `brand` (Pomegranate) | `#E63462` | Primary brand, main CTAs, logo, wheel accents |
| `brand-dark` | `#C21E4C` | Pressed / hover states |
| `chili` | `#F2542D` | Discounts, "ending soon", love/favourite |
| `sun` | `#FFD23F` | Highlights, stars, badges, confetti, the bite-dot ring |
| `lagoon` | `#0E8C99` | Games, mystery, achievements, secondary CTAs |
| `lime` | `#9BE15D` | Open now, verified, healthy, success |
| `rice` | `#FFF8EE` | Page background |
| `white` | `#FFFFFF` | Cards |
| `ink` (Deep plum) | `#2B1B3D` | Text, outlines, hard shadows |
| `ink-soft` | `#6B5B7B` | Secondary text |

Hierarchy rule: **one loud colour per card**. Discount = chili, verified = lime, game = lagoon, brand action = pomegranate.
Category cards keep their own soft food tints so the 23 categories stay easy to tell apart.

## 5. Typography
- **Display**: *Baloo Bhaijaan 2* (700–800) — rounded, friendly, Arabic + Latin in one family.
- **Body/UI**: *Rubik* (400–700) — rounded geometric, excellent Arabic + Latin.
- Prices use Latin digits with 3 decimals: `1.750 د.ك` / `KWD 1.750` (Kuwait convention).

## 6. Visual language — "Sticker Pop"
- Large rounded cards (24–32 px radius) with a **2 px plum outline + hard offset shadow** (`4px 4px 0`), like die‑cut stickers.
- Food photography in rounded frames, tilted ±2° in collages.
- Gradients only as "sauce": soft animated blobs behind heroes, never on text.
- Emoji‑illustration fallbacks when a photo is missing (never a broken image).
- Motion: springy and short. Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` + an in‑app toggle.

## 7. Voice & tone
| Situation | EN | AR |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | What are we eating today? | شنو ناكل اليوم؟ |
| Spin CTA | Spin the food wheel! | لِف عجلة الأكل! |
| Win | Dinner is decided! | انحسمت الوجبة! |
| Empty favourites | Nothing saved yet — go get hungry. | ما حفظت شي للحين.. يلا جوّع نفسك 😋 |
| No data | We don't know this yet — and we won't guess. | ما عندنا هالمعلومة.. وما راح نخمّن. |

Gulf‑friendly Arabic for headlines and buttons, clear Modern Standard Arabic for terms, prices and legal text.

## 8. Trust marks (part of the brand)
- 🟢 **Verified** — offer checked against the restaurant's official source.
- 🟡 **Community** — submitted by users, not yet verified.
- 🧪 **Demo data** — sample content while live integrations are not connected.
- 🗺️ **OpenStreetMap** — real place data from OSM contributors (no ratings invented).
- Every price/offer shows "last updated"; expired offers never show as active.
